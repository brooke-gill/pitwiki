The Pit 1.0.7
From the Hypixel Pit Wiki

The Pit 1.0.7, also known as the Bugs Patch, was a minor version of the Hypixel Pit. It was primarily composed of bug fixes and minor changes.

Bug fixes:

  • Refunded auctions no longer count towards prestige requirements.
  • Players can now say "gg" in chat whenever they want, instead of only for 7 seconds after a major event.
  • Standardized Sewer Rubbish lore so that older instances could stack with newly created Rubbish.
  • The Khanate killstreak now actually adds +8g to the kill or assist that triggered it.
  • Olympus Potions obtained from Death Streaks no longer give XP.
  • The Death Streak chat message no longer declares all Death Streak items as Golden Apples.
  • Players can no longer place blocks near the dragon egg.
  • Fixed the Pit Supporter star not appearing in chat at times. It will still not appear during a major event.
  • The Podium Position achievement and bonus gold from Self-Confidence can now be obtained from Blockhead.
  • Fixed players' remaining contracts for the day not being displayed if they had not completed any that day.
  • Fixed being able to spawn Devil Chicks using a Spire event could attack players inside. Players not actively participating can also no longer obtain Fractured Souls.
  • Participation XP is now capped at 10,000.
  • Players with Escape Pod can no longer die before it triggers.
  • The Explosive enchantment can no longer hit players in spawn.
  • Fixed an issue where players could not bid on an Auction if they had the exact required amount of gold.
  • Obtaining an Uberdrop now properly updates the inventory when playing on more recent versions of Minecraft.

Two minor changes were additionally made:

  • The Pizza event now gives players a 30-second advance warning before the event ends to turn in their cash.
  • Ender Pearls can no longer be used.

A follow-up update was released on May 11, 2026, assumed to be a continuation of the 1.0.7 patch due to being unnumbered and not acknowledged in-game. The following changes were made:

  • It is no longer possible to fish above Y=105.
  • Players are now limited to 100 Fishing catches per 24 hours, resetting at 12:00am Eastern Time. This can be increased up to 1,000 through the new Fish Maxxing renown upgrade.
  • The cooldown on Telebow can no longer be decreased by shooting yourself.
  • Players can no longer bid against themselves on Auctions by rejoining an instance.
  • Non-prestiged players can no longer bid more than 50,000g on Auctions.
